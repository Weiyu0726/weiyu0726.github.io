THE IDEA

Gesture is a dialogue,
not a solo act.

Conversation involves more than a speaker moving to their own voice. Both people speak, listen, respond, and express themselves in a characteristic way.

DialoGen jointly generates gestures for both interlocutors from conversational audio. An interactive dual-diffusion model coordinates their motions, while identity-decoupled style guidance preserves each person’s gestural traits.

01 / JOINT GENERATION

Both sides, together.

Two weight-sharing denoisers generate temporally aligned gesture sequences.

02 / MUTUAL INTERACTION

Motion that responds.

Estimated interaction weights model the influence between both participants.

03 / INDIVIDUAL STYLE

A sense of identity.

Contrastive style learning separates identity-specific traits from gesture content.
